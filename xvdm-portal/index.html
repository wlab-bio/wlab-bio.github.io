Contents

What the demonstration includes

The sample exercises the same browser views used for an xVDM reconstruction, while keeping the public preview separate from submitted experiments.

Reconstruction

3D node cloud

Rotate the spatial view, select reconstructed nodes, and inspect the inferred organization of the simulated specimen.

Context

Molecular neighborhoods

From a selected node, view its local UEI relationships, sub-consensuses, and molecular annotations.

Annotation

Gene mappings

Review alignment evidence produced with STAR against the validated zebrafish reference and extended rRNA annotation.

Requesting access

Membership in the access group is reviewed by the Weinstein Lab. Approved members sign in with their own Google account; no shared account is issued.

1. Request membership Open the access group and select “Ask to join.”
2. Wait for approval The request is reviewed by the portal owner.
3. Open the portal Return to this page and sign in with Google.
